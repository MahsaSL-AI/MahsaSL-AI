I'm an Applied AI Engineer focused on turning modern LLMs into useful, dependable products—not just demos.

My work sits at the intersection of agentic systems, retrieval-augmented generation, backend engineering, and intelligent automation. I care about clear system boundaries, human oversight, security, observability, and graceful failure when AI meets the real world.

- Building agentic systems with tool use, validation, and bounded decision loops

## Featured work

### [AI Sales Assistant for WooCommerce](https://github.com/mahsasl26-ui/ai-woocommerce-assistant-showcase)

A marketplace-ready conversational sales assistant that works with live WooCommerce catalog, inventory, variation, and cart data. Includes bounded agent behavior, provider abstraction, lead capture, human handoff, privacy controls, Persian localization, and RTL interfaces.

**Focus:** Agentic commerce · Tool calling · WordPress/WooCommerce · PHP · JavaScript · LLM safety

### [AI Email Approval Assistant](https://github.com/mahsasl26-ui/email-approval-assistant)

A production-minded email workflow that analyzes Gmail messages, prepares AI-assisted replies, and sends them only after explicit Telegram approval. Domain rules and persisted state prevent automation from bypassing human oversight.

**Focus:** Human-in-the-loop AI · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Telegram · Gmail · Docker

### [Knowledge Base RAG](https://github.com/mahsasl26-ui/knowledge-base-rag)

A grounded PDF knowledge-base application with vector search, page-level citations, source management, conversation history, and replaceable AI providers.

**Focus:** RAG · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · pgvector · OpenRouter · SQLAlchemy

### [Mahsa AI News Bot](https://github.com/mahsasl26-ui/mahsa-ai-news-bot)

A production-oriented n8n workflow that collects curated sources, generates validated Persian AI-news posts, and delivers them sequentially through Telegram.

**Focus:** Intelligent automation · n8n · Telegram · Google Sheets · OpenRouter · Docker
